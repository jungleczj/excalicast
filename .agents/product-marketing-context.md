## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Excalicast is a browser-based recording and editing workspace that turns whiteboards, browser tabs, app windows, or desktop capture into publish-ready video assets.

**What it does:** Users can capture a whiteboard or display source with microphone and camera, trim the result on a browser timeline, apply ChatCut-assisted silence and scene-aware edits, add editable Autozoom focus regions, generate captions and structured handouts, and export the same recording for multiple platforms. Excalicast produces publish-ready files and share links; it does not claim to upload directly to third-party social platforms.

**Product category:** Whiteboard recorder, browser screen recorder, online video editor, Excalidraw video recorder.

**Product type:** Local-first browser SaaS with optional cloud features.

**Business model:** Free watermarked export, one-time watermark-free export, and Pro/Max subscriptions.

## Target Audience

**Primary audiences:** Teachers, knowledge creators, software architects, and product managers.

**Primary use case:** Record a visual explanation and turn it into reusable, publish-ready video and knowledge assets without moving through several desktop tools.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Record a whiteboard, browser tab, app window, or desktop with voice and optional camera.
- Remove dead time, focus attention, and prepare the recording for viewers.
- Export one recording in the dimensions and supporting formats required by several publishing channels.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Visual explainers usually require separate capture, editing, captioning, reframing, and publishing-preparation tools.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Whiteboard-only recorders may stop at capture and download.
- Generic screen recorders capture pixels but do not preserve a whiteboard operation stream.
- General video editors add workflow and installation overhead for a focused explainer.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Excalicord, ExcaliRec, Explain Everything.

**Secondary:** Loom, Screen Studio, Tella, VEED, FocuSee, Screenity.

**Foundational canvas:** Excalidraw and tldraw.

All competitor statements must be sourced from public product documentation and dated. If a feature is not publicly documented, say that instead of claiming it is unsupported.

## Differentiation

- Multi-source capture: whiteboard, current tab, app window, or entire desktop.
- Browser timeline with trim, split, delete, and editable Autozoom focus regions.
- ChatCut-assisted silence removal with scene-aware alignment.
- Captions, chapter outline, structured handout, and share-link outputs on eligible tiers.
- One recording can be rendered into landscape, portrait, square, feed, and custom dimensions.
- Local-first recording and rendering, with explicit boundaries for opt-in cloud features.

## Objections And Boundaries

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| Is this just another whiteboard recorder? | It includes whiteboard recording, but the intended product is the complete capture-to-publish-ready workflow. |
| Does one-click publishing post directly to social platforms? | No. It means one workflow creates files and assets ready to publish; direct third-party posting is not claimed. |
| Is every feature fully automatic? | ChatCut can propose timeline cuts. Autozoom regions remain editable. Marketing copy must match verified product behavior. |

**Anti-persona:** Teams needing live classroom collaboration, heavy multi-track post-production, or automatic third-party social publishing.
