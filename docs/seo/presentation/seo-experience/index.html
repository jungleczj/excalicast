EXCALICAST TEST

Does this page make a recording, lesson, demo, or explanation easier to decide and make?

Yes → publish
No → keep researching
Speaker notes Top 3 research showed a repeatable shape: 45–58 character titles, a direct answer, 5–8 H2s, steps, proof, limits, and FAQs where they help. The recipe still needs original information gain. Otherwise it is just a very well-formatted sandwich.
